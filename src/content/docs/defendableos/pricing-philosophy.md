## The core principle

**DefendableOS is not priced like a website. It is priced like trust infrastructure.**

The website explains the asset. The app operates the asset. The receipts defend the asset. The deeds preserve the asset. The vocabulary compounds the asset.

## The engagement model

DefendableOS engagements run as **disposition assignments** under exclusive listing brokerage. Commission at closing. NOT hourly · NOT fixed fees.

See the [Operating Principles](/ecosystem/operating-principles/#the-disposition-vs-vendor-model) for the full vendor-vs-broker comparison.
