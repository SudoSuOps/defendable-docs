:::note[Roadmap]
The 5-Proof deed model — and this Origin proof — is DefendableLedger design intent, not a built or live capability. The live, verifiable provenance surface today is the DefendableCloud receipt: every Defendable Run records its inputs and timestamps and seals them into a per-org hash chain (see [Schemas · Receipt](/schemas/receipt/)).
:::

Proof of Origin answers **who** produced an artifact, **when**, and **in what context** — the non-negotiable first proof in the deed vision. The intended fields document the source event: speakers · participants · source_type · recorded_at · session_id. The point is that a deed should carry, on its face, enough provenance to reconstruct where the underlying work came from without trusting a narrator — the same books-and-records discipline DefendableCloud receipts already enforce at the run level.
