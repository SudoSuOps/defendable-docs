## The 7 lessons (from the founding origin story)

1. **Bring the math · not the complaint.** Every operator ask must come with receipts · numbers · the calculation laid bare.
2. **Digest before you react.** Emotional response is information · NOT a decision.
3. **Wtf is information · not a decision.** The decision comes AFTER the wtf.
4. **Take the unfair deal · then outgrind the position.** Use the grind to build the receipts that earn the better seat.
5. **PASS doctrine · refuse fantasy mandates.** "More hours" sold as "a raise" was the first fantasy mandate the founder ever learned to spot.
6. **MAGIC funnel discipline · count every engagement.** 1:500 conversion math from the origin story · directly.
7. **My math is better than his · hunt the better seat.** The CHAIR is the asset · NOT the grind. Different seats have different math.
