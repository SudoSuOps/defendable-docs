## The 4 tiers

| Tier | Score | What it means | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Royal Jelly** | ≥ 0.85 | Apex · publicly defensible · institutional-grade | Ships · feeds training corpus |
| **Honey** | 0.70 — 0.84 | Approved with confidence · operator-grade | Ships |
| **Jelly** | 0.50 — 0.69 | Repair candidate · close to good but not yet | Routes to SwarmFixer |
| **Propolis** | < 0.50 | Walk · do not ship · root-cause | Hold · investigate · PASS |

## The CRE Class A/B/C parallel

| Tribunal tier | CRE asset class |
|---|---|
| Royal Jelly | Class A trophy property · institutional-grade |
| Honey | Class A · prime · ships at full ask |
| Jelly | Class B · value-add · needs work to lease at market |
| Propolis | Class C · distressed · walk OR major reposition |

A broker who pretends a Class C is a Class A loses credibility. We do the same: we never call a Propolis-tier artifact Honey. The transparency IS the trust.

## False Honey

The classic adjudication failure mode: the agent sounded smart but failed the assignment. Caught by the validator chain. Downgrades the artifact. Publicly documented. Feeds SwarmFixer training. The most honest content we publish.
