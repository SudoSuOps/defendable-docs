:::note[Roadmap]
The Defendable Box is a **vision / roadmap appliance**, not a shipping product — nothing on this page is deployed. The live trust layer runs in **DefendableCloud** at `api.defendablecloud.com`.
:::

The Defendable Box is the premium tier of the edge roadmap: where HoneyBox is the always-on low-power node, the Defendable Box is the heavier appliance for operators who need real model capacity at the edge. The planned range spans Jetson Orin class (Nano / NX / AGX, roughly 4B through 14B–32B parameter capable) at the small end, up to full RTX 6000 server-class hardware at the top.

Per-vertical configurations (cold storage · logistics · industrial · supply chain · orchestrator) are **planned**, not available — the idea is one chassis concept tuned per vertical, but those configs are roadmap, not a menu you can order today.
