title: Risk Temperature
description: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk-temperature read. Triggered by risk-flag count and severity.
---

LOW: zero or one MEDIUM-severity risk flag. MEDIUM: 2+ MEDIUM flags OR 1 HIGH flag. HIGH: 2+ HIGH flags. Risk temperature is independent of classification · documented in every verdict record.
