## The signature opening

```
"Ring ring · hello dev · Mr. Defendable here · got a minute"
```

That phrase IS the intake. Every conversation starts there.

## MAGIC funnel translation to AI ops

| Stage | Operator meaning |
|---|---|
| **M** — Meetings | Direct outreach · principal-to-principal · NOT email · NOT chat |
| **A** — Appraisals | Pre-LOI conversations · price discovery · qualified buyer triage |
| **G** — Genuine interest | PASS doctrine applied · refuse tire-kickers |
| **I** — Ink | LOI · then PSA · then close |
| **C** — Close | DDEED title transfer · receipt · publicly verifiable |
