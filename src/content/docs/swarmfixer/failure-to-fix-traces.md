---
title: Failure → Fix Traces
description: The trace structure that proves a fix happened.
---

Every repair attempt produces a trace: failure_id · failure_mode · repair_directive · pre_score · post_score · repair_lift · time_to_fix. Traces are deeded as DDEED-REPAIR-* artifacts on Hedera.
