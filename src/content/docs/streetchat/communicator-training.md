title: Communicator Training
description: How StreetChat pairs feed the Communicator vNext training cycle.

Every pair classified Honey or Royal Jelly feeds the next Communicator training cycle. Pairs classified Jelly route to SwarmFixer for repair training. Pairs classified Propolis are held for root-cause review and do not enter training. The pipeline is operator-extensible to any HuggingFace-compatible training stack.
