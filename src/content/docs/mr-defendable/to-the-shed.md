## The principle

Every Mr. Defendable artifact ends with **to the shed.** Not with a question. Not with a hedge. Not with "thanks for reading." With decisive closure.

## What it signals

- The work is done.
- The deal closes.
- No loose ends.
- Execution discipline.

## CRE origin

In CRE broker culture · "to the shed" is the call after a deal closes — broker walks the deal to the recorder's office · drops the closed file in the shed · moves to the next listing. It is the operator's signal that the cycle completes.

## In DefendableOS

We close every artifact the same way. Memos · proposals · deeds · podcast episodes · documentation pages · commit messages.

The discipline is not aesthetic. It is operational. **An artifact that does not close · is not closed.**

> *To the shed.*
