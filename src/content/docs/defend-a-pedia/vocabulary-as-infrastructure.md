## The thesis

Operators talk in real language. Compliance · audit · and AI alignment all break when the language is fuzzy. By **deeding the vocabulary first** · we remove the fuzziness at the source.

- The model trains on the deeded definition.
- The validator scores against the deeded definition.
- The buyer reads the deeded definition.
- Everyone aligned to the same immutable record.

## What "deeded vocabulary" means in practice

Every term in Defend-A-Pedia is a `DDEED-VOCAB-*` artifact with:

- 13-section term schema (street definition · CRE meaning · DefendableOS definition · client explanation · backend representation · tribunal use · evidence required · failure modes · related terms · scoring impact · deed/receipt impact · source · hashes)
- SHA-256 hash over canonical JSON form
- Markdown source · also hashed
- ENS anchor at `defendapedia.eth/{category}/{slug}`
- Books-record anchor at `streetledger.eth/vocabulary/v0.3.0/{deed_id}`
- Hedera HCS anchor on topic `0.0.10291838`
