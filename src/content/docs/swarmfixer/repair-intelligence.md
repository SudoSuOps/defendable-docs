---
title: Repair Intelligence
description: What makes SwarmFixer different from just re-running the agent.
---

Re-running gets you the same failure. SwarmFixer extracts the failure mode · maps it to a known taxonomy · generates a targeted repair directive · re-runs with the directive prepended. Repair lift is measurable per failure mode.
