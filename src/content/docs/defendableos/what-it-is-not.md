title: What DefendableOS Is NOT
description: Disambiguation from generic AI SaaS, judge models, agent frameworks, and Web3 token projects.
---

## NOT another AI agent

DefendableOS does not compete with OpenAI · Anthropic · Cohere · or any agent framework. We are the **layer that runs after the agent finishes** — testing the work against a declared rulebook and issuing receipts.

## NOT another LLM platform

We use LLMs · but we are not selling LLMs. We are selling **receipts on what the LLMs did**.

## NOT a judge model

The referee is a **rulebook engine**, not an AI judge. There is no model grading the agent's work for quality on the receipt path. Every check passes or raises a flag against a declared rule. Score = % of declared rules satisfied, not an opinion score. Model assistance is advisory only — clearly separated, never on the chain. *(See [Rulebook Engine](/defendableos/rulebook-engine/) for the full doctrine.)*

## NOT another observability dashboard

Datadog tells you the model ran. We tell you whether the model actually completed the assignment against a declared rulebook · with what evidence · with what cost · and we issue a receipt you can take to a buyer or a regulator.

## NOT a token project

There is no token. No ICO. No staking. No yield farming. The trust layer is an **in-house per-org hash-chained ledger** — not a blockchain anchor. See the [Kill Hedera doctrine](/defendableledger/kill-hedera/): the house owns the rail end to end; trust compounds inside the books, not on someone else's chain.

## NOT external chain anchoring

Earlier ecosystem documentation referenced Hedera HCS for timestamping. As of 2026-05-24 that approach is **superseded for spine work**. New receipts join the per-org hash chain. Historical entries that mention Hedera anchoring remain in the books-and-records (they were true at the time), but new spine records do not anchor externally.

## NOT generic AI SaaS

Generic AI SaaS sells **velocity** · DefendableOS sells **proof**. Generic AI SaaS optimizes for **demos** · we optimize for **books and records that survive scrutiny**.

## NOT a website build

DefendableOS engagements are delivered as **disposition assignments** under a CRE-grade rep agreement (commission at closing, not hourly, not fixed fees, not vendor invoices). That's the *engagement model*. The *product* is the engine + the vault — see [DefendableCloud · Overview](/defendablecloud/overview/) for the hosted product surface.
