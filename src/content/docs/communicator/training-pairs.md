:::note[Roadmap]
The StreetChat → Communicator training-pair flywheel is **planned, not built**. It depends on StreetChat (also roadmap) and on the Communicator rail itself. This page describes the intended cadence · the Royal Jelly tier vocabulary it uses (honey / jelly / propolis) is real doctrine carried across the stack.
:::

The intended flow: each Honey / Royal-Jelly Communicator pair would become a supervised training example. The Communicator vNext model would be fine-tuned on the accumulated corpus on a rolling cadence. Pairs classified Propolis would be excluded from training until reviewed.
