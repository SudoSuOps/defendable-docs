## The senior-hack mandate

Senior hacks run the listing. Senior hacks bring the buyer pool. Senior hacks close the deal.

| Discipline | What it does |
|---|---|

| Protect the principal | The 5 Controls · always |

## The escalation path

Senior hacks who consistently close trophy assets become principals. The path is not promotion · it is **proven receipts** that change the math underneath the chair.
