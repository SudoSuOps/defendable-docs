:::note[Roadmap]
Proof of Economics is part of the DefendableLedger 5-Proof deed vision — design intent, not a built or live service. See the [Cost to Mint](/schemas/cost-to-mint/) vision page for the breakdown model.
:::

Proof of Economics answers **what an artifact actually cost to mint** — in dollars and in watts — so the price of trust is on the record instead of hand-waved. The intended fields: cost_to_mint_usd · a per-input breakdown (compute · human review · validator · storage · energy · retries · repair · deed issuance) · the training-pair value produced · and comparisons to the hyperscaler-equivalent and human-only-equivalent cost. The doctrine: everybody talks about intelligence; nobody puts what it costs to mint on the books. A deed should.
