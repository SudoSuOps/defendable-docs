## NOT another AI agent

DefendableOS does not compete with OpenAI · Anthropic · Cohere · or any agent framework. We are the **layer that runs after the agent finishes** · auditing what was done.

## NOT another LLM platform

We use LLMs · but we are not selling LLMs. We are selling **receipts on what the LLMs did.**

## NOT another observability dashboard

Datadog tells you the model ran. We tell you whether the model actually completed the assignment · with what evidence · with what cost · and we issue a deed you can take to a buyer or a regulator.

## NOT a token project

There is no token. No ICO. No staking. No yield farming. The platform is built on Hedera HCS for **immutable timestamping** · NOT for speculation.

## NOT generic AI SaaS

Generic AI SaaS sells **velocity** · DefendableOS sells **proof**. Generic AI SaaS optimizes for **demos** · we optimize for **books and records that survive scrutiny.**

## NOT a website build

Every engagement we run is a **disposition assignment** under a CRE-grade rep agreement. Commission at closing. NOT hourly · NOT fixed fees · NOT vendor invoices.
