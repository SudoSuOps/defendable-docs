title: Data Retention
description: How long DefendableOS retains different artifact classes.
---

Default retention: receipts and deeds (forever · books-and-records discipline). Raw audio (12 months · operator-configurable). Transcripts (7 years · operator-configurable). Personal-data fields obey deletion requests within 30 days.
