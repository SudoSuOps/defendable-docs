IRC §1031 (as amended by the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017) applies to REAL PROPERTY held for investment or productive use in trade or business. **Intangible AI / IP asset sales do NOT directly qualify.** Tax counsel + Qualified Intermediary own structure determination · NOT broker. Broker provides: sourcing · 45/180-day calendar discipline · QI coordination · DST/TIC backup. See [Operating Principles](/ecosystem/operating-principles/) for the full broker-vs-counsel scope split.
