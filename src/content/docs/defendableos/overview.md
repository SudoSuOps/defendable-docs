## What DefendableOS is

DefendableOS is the **defense layer** for AI agents · the trust operating system that captures AI work · runs it through the Tribunal · issues receipts · deeds the record · publishes to StreetLedger · and feeds the repair layer.

When AI offense fails — and it does · constantly — DefendableOS is the layer that says **what happened · why · what failed · what got repaired · and what we trained the next model on.**

## Why it exists

> *"Offense goes dark. The business is offline. It can't score."*

Most AI platforms build offense. Few build defense. We build defense.
