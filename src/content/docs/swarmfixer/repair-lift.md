---
title: Repair Lift
description: The pre-vs-post verdict delta. The planned operator KPI for the repair layer.
---

:::note[Roadmap]
Repair lift is the **planned** KPI for SwarmFixer. It is not yet measured by any
deployed service.
:::

Repair lift = `post_score - pre_score`. It would be measured per failure mode · per
agent · per repair version.

Repair lift IS the moat: agents that get repaired better stay deployed longer. That
is the operator framing — but until SwarmFixer is built, no service computes this
number.
