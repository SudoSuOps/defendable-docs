## The 8 voice rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Principal-led (NOT corporate-led) | A person speaks · not a brand machine |
| CRE-broker cadence | 30-year operator credibility · NOT tech-startup energy |
| Plain English over jargon | Operator-readable · NOT benchmark-readable |
| Confidence without bravado | Class A 5-cap discipline · NOT hype |
| Specific over generic | Names · numbers · receipts · NOT "world-class" |
| Receipts over claims | Every assertion resolves to a verifiable source |
| "To the shed" close | Deals close · no loose ends · execution discipline |
| Founder voice preservation | V03 validator chain on every surface |

## Banned phrases (V03 auto-block · sample)

```
world-class · best-in-class · transformational · leverage synergies ·
drive efficiencies · actionable insights · AI-powered · AI-driven ·
next-generation · revolutionary · disruptive · paradigm shift
```
