---
title: Consent for Recordings
description: Per-jurisdiction consent requirements for capturing audio sessions.
---

Single-party vs two-party consent jurisdictions documented. It is the operator's responsibility to confirm consent before any audio is captured or ingested — the platform does not assume consent on your behalf.

:::note[Roadmap · StreetChat consent flag]
A consent flag captured at session-open and stored on the session/event record is planned for the StreetChat capture lane. StreetChat is not deployed today, so treat this as roadmap — not current ingest behavior. Confirm and record consent through your own process until it ships.
:::
