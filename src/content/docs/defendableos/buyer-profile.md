## The 5 buyer personas

| Persona | Care list | Their question |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder / CEO / Principal** *(BEST · 60% close)* | Business risk · reputation · revenue interruption | *"If my AI fails · who knows first · what proof do I have?"* |
| **COO / Head of Operations** *(STRONG · 45%)* | Workflow reliability · escalation paths | *"Can I trust these agents to operate without chaos?"* |
| **General Counsel / Compliance** *(VERY STRONG in regulated · 55%)* | Audit trail · explainability · liability | *"Can we defend this AI decision later?"* |
| **CFO** *(ECONOMIC GATE · 30%/80%)* | Cost to mint · risk-adjusted ROI · vendor accountability | *"What is the cost of bad AI output versus verified AI output?"* |
| **AI Product Owner** *(TECH CHAMPION · 70% tech / 10% signature)* | Model performance · failure modes · benchmark lift | *"How do we know our agents are completing assignments?"* |

## The 8 vertical targets (close-probability ranked)

| Vertical | Why they buy |
|---|---|
| Healthcare | AI mistakes create compliance · patient · billing · trust risk |
| Finance / Credit | Decisions require records · scoring · defensibility |
| Insurance | Claims · underwriting · evidence trails matter |
| Legal | Letters · disputes · filings · compliance need proof |
| CRE / Asset Valuation | Underwriting · comps · opinions need books and records |
| AI SaaS Companies | They need to prove their agents work |
| Customer Support Automation | Brand/revenue damage from bad agent responses |
| Compute / GPU Marketplaces | Provenance · uptime · workload trust matter |

## Clean ICP (qualification gate)

1. Founder-led OR compliance-sensitive company
2. $2M – $100M revenue (M&M sweet spot)
3. Using AI agents · or preparing to deploy
4. Workflows where bad AI output creates risk
5. Needs receipts · validation · repair · reporting
6. Wants ownership-level confidence

## The close

> *"The AI agent is now part of my business. I need books and records on what it does."*

That sentence · in any form · means the deal is closed.
